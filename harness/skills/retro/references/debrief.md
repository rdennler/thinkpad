# Debrief Flow & Question Bank

Tone: curious interviewer, not evaluator. Ground every question in something you read in the files ("On Jun 3 you logged 'REQUIRE alone can't prove the node was stored'. What made that click?"). Ask 1–2 questions per turn. Touch all four lanes before writing anything.

## Opening

- "Looking at the last N sessions since <date>, what stands out to you first, before I show you what I see?"
- Then a 3-line factual recap from `progress.md` (dates, units covered) so you're both looking at the same period.

## Lane 1 — What clicked

- "You marked <X> as clicked on <date>. Explain it now, cold, in one sentence." (retention probe)
- "What was different about the session where it landed: the example, the question, the mode?"
- "Which mnemonic or analogy did you actually reach for?"

## Lane 2 — What was hard

- "Where did a session stall? Was it *concept* stuck, *procedure* stuck, or *motivation* stuck?"
- "Anything logged 'still fuzzy' twice or more? That's a pattern. What's the common thread?"
- "Was there a moment you wanted the answer handed over? What would a better hint have looked like?"

## Lane 3 — What strategy emerged

- "Did you catch yourself doing a repeatable move worth naming?"
- "If you could teach last month's you one trick, what is it?"
- "Should that become a mnemonic, a reframe, or a new exercise?"

## Lane 4 — What should change about the environment

- "Where did the environment itself create friction: file formats, session flow, the hint ladder, difficulty, scheduling?"
- "What did you work around manually more than once?"
- "If one rule in the env contract could change, which one, and to what?"
- Push each answer to a **concrete proposal**: target file plus specific change. That's what goes in the queue.

## Closing

- Read back the draft Learnings and Proposed Changes; get a yes or an edit on each.
- Confirm which learnings and mnemonics are durable enough for the graph. Skip one-offs.
- Then, and only then, write the retro file, the queue items, and the graph appends.
